Why Track
Bowlers?

Cricket analysis has long been dominated by batting stats — strike rates, centuries, and run tally. BowlerStats.in flips the script. We believe the real story of an IPL match is told through the bowler's economy rate and the pressure they exert.

Every over matters. A tight powerplay from a pace bowler can define the entire chase. Our over-by-over histogram makes it instantly visible — which bowlers leaked runs, which ones took wickets at crucial moments.

  • Live over-by-over histogram updated after each over
  • RCB and MI bowling comparison in one view
  • Economy rate colour-coded: green, amber, red
  • Wicket overs highlighted with 🎯 marker
  • Automatically deployed via GitHub → Vercel pipeline
  • No app needed — works perfectly on mobile browser
